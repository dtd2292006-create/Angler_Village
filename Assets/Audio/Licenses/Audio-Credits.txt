MODIFICATIONS
Clips were renamed, decoded/resampled to 44.1 kHz, level-conditioned,
and trimmed. Short one-shots have small edge fades. Loop clips have an
overlap crossfade at the boundary. Ambiences use 45-second source sections.
Music and continuous loops were encoded as Ogg Vorbis; one-shots are PCM WAV.
The casting whoosh adapts Kenney's knifeSlice.ogg foley; the line snap adapts
pluck_001.ogg. No original authored soundtrack or exclusive rights are claimed.

AudioManifest.json records each final asset, original filename, source page,
license choice, processing, duration and source/output SHA-256 hashes.

COMPACT VERSION
Only the 19 core cues are shipped. Background music uses 24-second mono
22050 Hz excerpts with short edge fades; these excerpts repeat in-game.
No shop, commission or progression audio adapters are added in this version.
